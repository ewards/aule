# Cross-domain set

Purpose: import operating patterns from unrelated domains that already
solve an analogous problem at scale.

## How to apply

1. Restate the problem's core loop in one domain-neutral sentence: who
   does what with what under what pressure.
2. Pick three unrelated domains that have solved that loop for decades.
   Good defaults: airport or hospital operations, auctions and markets,
   kitchens and food service, game design, insurance underwriting,
   logistics and warehousing, orchestras and ensembles, search and
   rescue. Choose domains that fit the loop; do not force the defaults.
3. For each domain: name the operating pattern (slot times and stand
   assignment, double-entry bookkeeping, mise en place, blind bidding,
   actuarial premium pools), then map it onto the problem: what is the
   slot, the ledger, the mise, the bid in this build?
4. Keep the 3-5 mappings that produce a concrete, buildable change rather
   than a metaphor. A candidate that cannot be demoed in 48 hours is a
   metaphor - drop it.

## Output

3-5 candidates. Each: a one-line title, the source domain and pattern,
one line of the mapping, one line of kill-risk.
