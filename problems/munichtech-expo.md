## Problem statement

"Build What Europe Needs." A Europe-focused innovation challenge for developers, researchers, students, startups, and industry professionals: build practical, high-impact, deployable technology (not demos that end on Sunday night) across AI & applied ML, digital sovereignty & European tech infrastructure, Industry 4.0/manufacturing/supply chains, digital health & public services, sustainability/energy/climate tech, and data platforms/automation/enterprise software. Selected projects are showcased at MunichTech EXPO before enterprises, investors, and ecosystem partners.

## Context

- Host: MunichTecg EXPO; Munich, Germany (public); deadline Sep 20, 2026 @ 5:00pm CEST; ~81 participants.
- Eligibility: above legal age of majority; teams required, 2–6 members; all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions.
- Submission: working prototype or functional PoC (software/hardware/hybrid — must demonstrate functionality); project description (problem, solution, target industry, why it matters for Europe); technical details (architecture, stack, AI models/datasets/APIs); repository or live demo link; 2–3 min demo video.
- Prizes (non-cash, 5 awards): MunichTech EXPO Grand Challenge Award; Best Applied AI Solution; Best Industry & Enterprise Use Case; Best Sustainability & Societal Impact Solution; Best Student/Early Talent Project — on-stage ceremony, EXPO showcase slot, website/press/social visibility, introductions to industry partners/investors/public sector, fast-track to exhibiting or the Talent Hub.
- Judging: Problem Relevance & Impact; Technical Excellence & Feasibility; Innovation & Originality; Practical Applicability & Scalability; Presentation & Communication.
