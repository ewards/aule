## Problem statement

"Solving Problems That Matter Most" (IDEATE. INNOVATE. IMPACT.) — a problem-driven online hackathon by the Rotaract Club of SNS College of Technology, choosing from 10 problem statements across **AI** (intelligent business document analysis; predictive food-waste management), **Automation** (end-to-end event management automation; dynamic queue management), **Cloud Computing** (scalable real-time application infrastructure), **Finance & Economics** (real-time digital fraud detection; intelligent scholarship matching; 30-day small-business cash-flow prediction), **Healthcare Technology** (intelligent healthcare service navigation — a coordination tool, not a diagnostic system), or **Open Innovation** (any validated real-world problem within those five themes).

## Context

- Round 1 (free): submit solution PPT + pitch video on Devpost, Aug 27 – Sep 26, 2026 (deadline Sep 26 @ 11:00pm IST); 92 participants; teams of 2–4; ages 13+; students only; specific countries/territories included.
- Round 2 (₹50/person, shortlisted teams only): build a functional prototype, Oct 5–14; final judging Oct 18–23; winners announced Oct 26.
- Prizes: ₹2,500 cash (Impact Champion ₹1,250; 1st runner-up ₹750; 2nd runner-up ₹500) + 5 Outstanding Participant Awards + 100 Certificates of Participation.
- Round 1 judging: clarity of problem identification/validation, understanding of affected users, innovative and feasible solution, potential real-world impact via PPT + pitch video.
- Community: official registration form (Google Form), Discord + WhatsApp communities required for Round 2 invitations.
