## Problem statement

"Got an idea for a better city? Build it." Smart City Hackathon 2026 (Lahore): turn real urban challenges into practical solutions under one of three themes — **Clean City** (software/hardware/data solutions to improve waste management, reduce pollution, enhance air quality), **City Intelligence** (software, AI, maps, and data tools that turn existing/open city data into insights, planning tools, predictive solutions, and citizen-facing information), and **Public Preparedness Ahead of the Climate Crisis** (communication and behaviour-change solutions for heatwaves, floods, smog, and other urban risks — short films, documentaries, campaigns, digital content, or other creative media allowed).

## Context

- Host: Code for Pakistan; venue Lahore Garrison University (Sector C DHA Phase 6, Lahore); sponsors: Code for Pakistan, Canva, Lahore Garrison University.
- Deadline Sep 1, 2026 @ 11:45pm PKT; ~163 participants; $642 cash (1st $108 × 3, 2nd $70 × 3, 3rd $36 × 3).
- Eligibility: above legal age of majority in country of residence; only specific countries/territories included. Individuals or teams.
- Submission: working prototype or demonstration (or creative output); project description (problem, why it matters, how it works, beneficiaries, impact/scalability); technology/innovation component explanation; demo video ≤ 3 min; presentation deck; source files/repository for tech projects.
- Judging: problem relevance, civic impact, innovation, technical execution, user experience, scalability, and clarity of communication.
- Judges: Dr Zillay Mariam (ISP Environmental Solutions), Abid Hussainy (The Urban Unit), Dr Ahmad Ali Gul (UMT Center for Disaster Management).
