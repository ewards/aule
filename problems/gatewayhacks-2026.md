## Problem statement

Build software, apps, and AI that open doors to a better future. GatewayHacks 2026 is the flagship virtual hackathon by the Gateway nonprofit: build a working software, hardware, or AI prototype that solves a real, documented problem faced by a community, nonprofit, or educational institution, and pick one of four Social Good Cause Tracks — Track 1 Accessibility & Health (empower people with disabilities, improve mental-health access, democratize healthcare info), Track 2 Equity in Education (bridge the digital divide, personalize learning, connect students with mentors), Track 3 Environmental Sustainability (climate action, conservation, green tech, circular economy), or Track 4 Open Impact & Community (civic engagement, financial empowerment, broad social impact). A partner-driven dynamic track additionally rewards the Best No-Code AI App built with Momen (momen.app).

## Context

- Online, public; $591,585 total prize value (cash + sponsor credits); 4-week virtual event, Sep 1 – Oct 2, 2026; deadline Oct 2, 2026 @ 12:00am EDT.
- Eligibility: ages 13+ only; students only; companies/professional organizations excluded; all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions.
- Solo or teams up to 4; joining the official Gateway Discord is mandatory (find teammates, mentors, updates).
- Submission: Devpost project page (title, short description of the problem solved, at least one visual); video pitch max 5 min (YouTube link — working prototype, no-code app, or design presentation); optional GitHub repo or live site link.
- Judging: Social Impact 40% (solves a real-world problem effectively); Technical Execution 30% (prototype/no-code app/design functions as intended); Innovation 20% (creative, unique, original approach); Design & UX 10% (intuitive, visually appealing, accessible).
- Prizes: Best Overall $2,100 cash (+ $2,000 Momen credits, $50 cash, $50 AOPS coupon); 2nd $2,050 (+ $2,000 credits, $25 cash, $25 AOPS); 3rd $2,035 (+ $2,000 credits, $10 cash, $25 AOPS); 4th $2,000 (+ $2,000 credits); Best No-Code AI App (Momen) $2,000 cash + $2,000 credits; 100 free .xyz domains ($15 each); $100 Momen credits × 500 (Discord); $299 Tin Computer Growth Credits × 100 (first-come); $1,000 Adaption credits × 500.
- Host: GatewayGS (Gateway nonprofit). Sponsors: Art of Problem Solving, Gen.xyz, Momen, Tin Computer. Judges: Jonathan Chang (GatewayGS CTO), Yaokai Jiang (Momen).
