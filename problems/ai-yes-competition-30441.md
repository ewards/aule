## Problem statement

AI YES! EVERY YES! The 1st International Youth AI Competition by the International AI Youth Education Society (AIYES), a global non-profit for youth AI education: use AI to solve real-world problems through apps, proposals, creative images, or videos. Four tracks: Track 1 AI Application Development (design, build, and deploy a practical AI-powered app — problem definition, data handling, model selection/integration, development, testing, UX); Track 2 AI Innovation Proposal (an original, forward-thinking AI proposal with problem statement, approach, technical feasibility, impact, and ethics); Track 3 AI Generative Image Storytelling (a coherent AI-generated image series, up to 10 images, narrating a full story with captions); Track 4 AI Generative Video Maker (an original 3–5 min AI-made video using script generation, image/text-to-video, animation, VFX, voice/music generation, editing).

## Context

- Online, public; 3 non-cash prizes (no cash); deadline Sep 1, 2026 @ 12:00pm PDT (Sep 1, 3:00pm EDT); Fall session — competition runs twice a year (Spring deadline Mar 1, Fall Sep 1).
- Eligibility: ages 13–18 only; students only (elementary, middle, and high school, all nationalities); team required (2–5 members) per Devpost tags — organizer text also allows individual participation; companies/professional organizations excluded; all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions.
- Submissions per track: Track 1 — slide presentation (development journey, innovation, impact, constraints), video walkthrough & live demo ≤ 5 min, source code or deployment link. Track 2 — written innovation proposal document, slide deck, video presentation ≤ 5 min. Track 3 — AI-generated image series (≤ 10), slide deck, video walkthrough ≤ 5 min. Track 4 — core AI video (3–5 min), slide deck, video walkthrough ≤ 5 min.
- Judging rubric: Problem Definition & Real-World Relevance (clear problem, strong need, defined users/impact); AI Technical Design & Model Strategy (appropriate model selection/integration, architecture clarity, depth beyond simple API use); User Experience & Design (intuitive interface, usability, user-centered thinking); track-specific criteria (narrative clarity, originality, visual coherence, responsible use of generative AI).
- Prizes: non-cash only — Gold/Silver/Bronze/Honorable Mention certificates (50 gold-silver-bronze pool; 5 Silver; 9 Bronze per Devpost prize list).
- Host: International AI Youth Education Society (AIYES). Judges include AI/IT leaders from Microsoft, AWS, Amazon, Deloitte, and academia.
