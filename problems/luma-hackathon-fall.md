## Problem statement

"Pitch your idea, win subscription prizes guaranteed!" Open-ended build hackathon: execute a functional project — an app, website, or software tool — that solves a real-world problem through code. The competition begins September 20 (submissions before that date are not considered). Round 1: submit a 5-minute video demo (Problem, Build, Demo, Scalability). Round 2: the top technical projects advance to a Live Demonstration and Q&A where finalists run their code live for judges.

## Context

- Host: LUMA (online, public); deadline Sep 28, 2026 @ 5:00pm EDT; ~98 participants.
- Eligibility: ages 13–18 only (middle school/high school students); companies excluded; all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions; teams of 1–5.
- Prizes: non-cash — sponsorship subscriptions for everyone (all 1,000 participants).
- Judging (25% each): Technical Execution (clean/efficient/documented code, works, stack grasp); Innovation & UX (original idea, intuitive interface, seamless flow); Business & Finance (realistic business model, revenue streams, sustainability); Communication (video + live Q&A show deep understanding of product, architecture, consumers).
