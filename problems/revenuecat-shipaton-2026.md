## Problem statement

Ship a brand-new app to the App Store, Google Play Store, or — new this year — the Samsung Galaxy Store, between August 1 and September 30, 2026. Integrate the RevenueCat SDK to power at least one in-app purchase or serve ads through RevenueCat Ads. Updates to previously released apps do not qualify — the app must be released for the first time during the Shipaton window.

## Context

- Online, public; $740,000+ cash prizes (~$1M total); deadline Sep 30, 2026 @ 11:45pm PDT (Oct 1, 2:45am EDT).
- Apps must be built for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, or Android; first public version released between Aug 1 and Sep 30, 2026.
- Submission: text description, demo video ≤ 2 min (public on YouTube/Vimeo), URL to the published app, 1024×1024 icon, screenshot at 1179×2556 with no device frame, and a free trial or promo code so judges can test premium features.
- Judging: Grand Prize ($100k) goes to the app gaining the most user traction and growth momentum during the event. Category awards are judged per category, e.g. Next Gen Award (students, judged on a video submission and open-source code), Catvertising Award (most creative/effective use of RevenueCat Ads), Best Game Award (gameplay, art direction, monetization fit), #BuildInPublic Award (most compelling public development journey), HAMM Award (most robust and creative monetization strategy), Design Award (craft of app development), Peace Prize (greatest social good), plus sponsor awards (JetBrains Kotlin Multiplatform cross-platform quality, Noise viral content, Samsung Galaxy optimization, Replit week-over-week revenue growth, OneSignal retention, Stripe web funnel).
- Ages 13–99; specific countries/territories excluded.
- Sponsors: Replit, OneSignal, JetBrains, Layers, Noise, Stripe, Samsung Galaxy Store, and others.
