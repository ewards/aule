## Problem statement

World Cup 2026 HACK: How can US cities optimize their sustainability performance (resource optimization) for hosting large sporting/general events (meetings, incentives, conferences, exhibitions), leveraging FIFA World Cup 2026 as a specific case? Four tracks: (1) Transportation & Access — a FIFA 2026 Host City Mobility Readiness Platform that predicts visitor movement, identifies first/last-mile gaps, compares transit resilience across the 11 US host cities, and recommends investments, transit solutions, and traffic management that improve access, cut emissions, and relieve congestion; (2) Energy–Food–Water Nexus — a Resource Intelligence Platform quantifying and visualizing the energy–food–water footprint of visitors, venues, hotels, and districts across all 11 US host cities, identifying highest-impact interventions; (3) Public Health & the Built Environment — map where visitor activity, urban heat islands, and climate risks overlap to support heat mitigation and public health interventions during FIFA 2026; (4) High-Intensity Corridors & Future Growth Districts — a data-driven classification system identifying and mapping urban corridors, districts, and communities by economic intensity, mobility patterns, land use, and development potential.

## Context

- Online, public; $17,500 cash; deadline Sep 17, 2026 @ 11:45pm CDT (Sep 18, 12:45am EDT); kickoff June 23–24, 2026.
- Eligibility: ages 18+ only; college students only; team required (2–6 members); companies/professional organizations excluded; US only.
- Optional 2-day in-person UNLEASH kickoff workshop at The ION, 4201 Main St, Houston TX (Jun 23–24, 8am–5pm, meals provided); teams guided through the UNLEASH Innovation Methodology (user-centered design/innovation approach); graders can join the UNLEASH practitioner network.
- Deliverables: pitch a solution idea that helps US cities optimize sustainability performance while hosting large events (FIFA 2026 case); develop an interactive data visualization (maps, charts, scenario comparisons) showing how urban sustainability strategies affect citywide outcomes in the chosen track — enabling decision-makers and residents to explore tradeoffs, identify priority areas, and understand measurable impact over time.
- Judging: Impact (sustainability, resilience, public health, mobility, resource efficiency, economic development, quality of life); Data Analytics (statistically sound, spatially accurate, evidence-supported); Innovation (novel approach/insight/model/visualization/classification/decision-support); Feasibility/Implementation (implementable by cities, agencies, venue operators, transit providers, developers, organizers); Legacy (benefits beyond FIFA 2026); Visualization (clear, compelling, decision-useful); Presentation/Pitch (challenge, methodology, findings, recommendations clearly communicated).
- Prizes: Grand Prize $10,000; Second $5,000; Third $2,500.
- Host: Rice University (with UNLEASH, FIFA26 Inc. participation). Judges include Rice faculty (Natural Sciences, CEE, Business, BioSciences), UNLEASH managing director, HGAC, AxVe, Sustainable Cities Group, FIFA26 Head of Environment.
