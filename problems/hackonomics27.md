## Problem statement

The official Hackonomics™ 2027 Hackathon: an experience to educate and grow insight into Computer Science and Economics. Create an innovative project, product, business, or idea that spreads awareness about financial literacy and economics in the community — open-ended and beginner-friendly, with submissions covering a wide range of economic or financial topics, in all mediums and at any stage of development (designs and prototypes permitted).

## Context

- Online, public; 5 non-cash prizes (current pool: 7 × 1-year .xyz domains); event window Jul 1, 2026 – Jun 1, 2027; deadline Jun 1, 2027 @ 5:00pm EDT.
- Eligibility: ages 13+ only; students only; companies/professional organizations excluded; specific countries/territories excluded.
- Submission: YouTube or Google Drive video describing the project (1–2 min recommended, 5 min max) plus a document of the source program (any medium).
- Judging: Relevancy (addresses economics/financial-literacy theme fused with code, educates others); Technical Execution (built well, proficiency, scalability, efficiency, soundness); Presentation (visually appealing, user-friendly, intuitive UI, thoughtful design); Impact (meaningful applications beyond the hackathon, long-term user/business/societal value); Innovation (unique/creative approach, novel ideas or technologies).
- Prizes: 1st/2nd/3rd place (1 × 1yr .xyz domain each, most effective at satisfying criteria); Design Award × 2 (sleek, user-friendly, polished design, max accessibility — Figma-based OK); Technical Award × 2 (best data and integration, comprehensive app).
- Host: hackonomics (community-run; previous editions 2024–2026). Sponsor: .xyz. Judges from AWS, Stripe, Meta, Samsung, Chime, Adobe, Venmo, Atlassian, Procore, and others.
