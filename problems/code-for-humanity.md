## Problem statement

Build technology that reaches the people who need it the most. Code for Humanity is a global online hackathon (beginner-friendly, open to first-time builders) that empowers innovators to build technology for social good: create a product that empowers people who are often overlooked — improving quality of life, expanding access to opportunities, or removing barriers for underserved communities such as disaster-response contexts, low-income or rural areas, people with disabilities, refugees and displaced populations, under-resourced schools, and regions with limited access to healthcare, education, digital technology, or financial services.

## Context

- Online, public; 5 non-cash prizes (certificates); event window Jul 16, 2026 – Jan 15, 2027; deadline Jan 15, 2027 @ 5:00pm PKT (7:00am EST).
- Eligibility: above legal age of majority in country of residence; students only; companies/professional organizations excluded; all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions. Solo or teams.
- Submission: demo video max 3 min; link to public GitHub (or similar) repo; all code must be written during the hackathon period (open-source libraries allowed).
- Judging: Impact 30% (solves a real-world problem); Innovation 25% (creative, unique, novel); Technical Excellence 20% (implementation quality, engineering, functionality); User Experience & Accessibility 15% (ease of use, intuitive design, accessibility); Scalability & Sustainability 10% (can it grow and keep creating value).
- Prizes: non-cash — Certificate of participation (999); Best Beginner Project; Most Innovative Project; Most Practical Project; Best Overall Project.
- Host: nill (organizer listed as "nill"; contact via COMSATS University Lahore email).
