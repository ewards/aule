## Problem statement

"Build Intelligence." HyperBloom Hacks September AI/ML edition: an online AI/ML hackathon for high school and college students to build intelligent solutions to real-world problems. From AI-powered applications and ML models to intelligent agents and creative experiments — AI/ML must be at the core of the solution. No restrictions on what you build; you don't need to train your own model (existing models and AI/ML APIs allowed as long as AI/ML plays a meaningful role).

## Context

- Host: hyperbloom hacks (online, public); deadline Sep 14, 2026 @ 5:00pm EDT; ~132 participants.
- Eligibility: above legal age of majority; students only; companies excluded; all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions. Solo or teams.
- $710 in prizes: 1st $260 cash + 2yr CodeCrafters VIP; 2nd $180 + 1yr VIP; 3rd $120 + 6mo VIP; Top 10 $15 + 1yr .xyz custom domain each. Sponsors: CodeCrafters, .xyz Domains.
- Submission: project description (200–500 words), GitHub repository link (or approved alternative), team member list, AI tools disclosure, post-event survey feedback.
- Judging: Impact & Relevance 25%; Innovation & Creativity 20%; Technical Implementation 25%; AI/ML Integration 20% (central, not an added feature); Presentation & Demo 10%.
