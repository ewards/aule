## Problem statement

Create The Ideas, Connect The Future. Theme: the "Web3 Frontier" — build decentralized solutions that leverage blockchain, advanced data security, and transparent architectures to solve real-world industrial problems. Participants build a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) addressing a specific industry problem using Web3 foundations (blockchain, smart contracts, decentralized storage), web-based/mobile/platform-integrated; AI and IoT stacks encouraged as long as core Web3 features are functional.

## Context

- Online, public; $1,247 cash; two-phase hybrid: Phase 1 conceptual online (proposal PDF, deadline Sep 18, 2026 @ 12:00am WIB / Sep 17 1:00pm EDT); Phase 2 — Top 30 teams do a 24-hour coding marathon on-site at President University or virtually for international teams.
- Eligibility: ages 15–25 only; students only (categories: college, high school, or international student); companies/professional organizations excluded; all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions. Teams of exactly 3 members.
- Submission Phase 1 (all): Proposal Idea Document PDF (max 10MB) using the mandatory official template (non-conforming submissions disqualified). Phase 2 (finalists): public GitHub/GitLab repo (commit history audited for the 24h window); presentation slides ≤ 10 (PDF/PPT); optional live deployment link (cloud/testnet/Vercel/Netlify) for bonus points.
- Judging (online phase): Innovation & Value Proposition 30%; Problem Relevance & Solution Fit 25%; Technical Architecture & Feasibility 20%; Market & Impact Viability 15%; Document Clarity & Structure 10%. Offline phase: Functional Project & Live Demo 35%; Problem-Solution Fit & Public Impact 30%; Creative Tech-Implementation 20% (incl. commit audit); Pitching & Q&A Defense 15%.
- Prizes: Grand Prize $500; 1st Runner Up $312; 2nd Runner Up $187; Public's Favourite $62 (most votes on compsphere.id); Best Pitching $62; Best Innovative Web3 $62; Best High School $62.
- Host: President University Faculty Association Computer Science (COMPSPHERE / HackSphere 2026). Sponsors: Featherless AI, NordVPN, NordPass, Saily, Incogni, Cloura. Judges include an AI/ML Engineer at Mattel.
