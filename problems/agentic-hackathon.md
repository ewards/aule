## Problem statement

A nationwide Bangladesh hackathon to build useful AI products with Devin: teams start online (round begins Aug 23), build a useful AI-powered software product that solves a real problem — web apps, mobile apps, developer tools, productivity tools, internal tools, community tools, or any working product where agentic AI makes the experience meaningfully better — and the strongest projects advance to a full-day in-person final at BRAC University, Dhaka (Sep 20) with live Devin sessions, final build sprints, demos, and awards.

## Context

- Host: Devin; final venue BRAC University (Dhaka, Bangladesh); online + in-person final; ~62 participants.
- Eligibility: anyone in Bangladesh (not limited to university students); above legal age of majority; teams of 3–4; only registered teams eligible.
- Timeline: registration Aug 15; online round Aug 23; submissions close Sep 6, 2026 @ 11:45pm BDT; judging Sep 7–11; finalists announced Sep 12; final Sep 20.
- Submission: working project link or deployed demo; short demo video/screen recording; short written summary (problem, solution, target users, how it uses AI/Devin); repository link if shareable; setup instructions.
- Prizes: confirmed pool BDT 60,000 (1st BDT 30,000; 2nd BDT 20,000; 3rd BDT 10,000), room to grow (Devpost lists $250).
- Judging focus: usefulness/clarity of problem; quality & completeness of working product; creativity & strength of AI/agentic workflow; technical execution & demo quality; potential impact for real users.
