## Problem statement

Build an educational tool that leverages the power of AI/Machine Learning to transform how people learn, teach, or absorb information — make knowledge more accessible, engaging, or personalized. Part of the SPEED virtual challenge series at Prometheus (beginner-friendly, open to AI enthusiasts, data scientists, and creative designers).

## Context

- Online, public; $1,500+ in prizes (cash + certificates); deadline Sep 3, 2026 @ 11:45am PDT (page says submit by Aug 29; Devpost deadline Sep 3).
- Solo or teams up to 4 members.
- Submission: 2-minute demo video showcasing the project; source code (GitHub repository, etc.).
- Judging (25 pts each): Educational Impact (solves a real problem in education; genuinely helps learn/teach/understand); Creative Use of AI/ML (AI core to functionality, not an afterthought); Technical Execution (functional, stable, intuitive; codebase quality, UI/UX); The Pitch & Demo (clear, concise, engaging 2-min video explaining why and how).
- Prizes: 1st $1,000 + certificate; 2nd $400 + certificate; 3rd $100 + certificate; 4th–5th certificates; 6th–10th certificates.
- Eligibility: ages 13+; students only; companies/professional organizations excluded; all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions.
- Host: SPEED / Prometheus.
