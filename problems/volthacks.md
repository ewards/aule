## Problem statement

Design. Build. Power the Future. VoltHacks 2026 is a hackathon focused on building real-world solutions using hardware, IoT, and AI — go beyond traditional software projects and create systems that interact with the physical world through sensors, embedded systems, automation, and intelligent devices. Whether robotics, smart devices, sustainability, healthcare technology, or open innovation: push creativity and engineering skills toward solving meaningful problems. Tracks could include Robotics & Embedded Systems, AI + Hardware Integration, Smart Health Technology, Sustainability & Smart Cities, Open Innovation, and anything engineering-based with impact. Projects can be hardware-based, software-based, or a combination of both.
