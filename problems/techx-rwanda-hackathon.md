## Problem statement

"Rethinking Career Growth in a Digital Age" — TechX 2026 is a thematic, 3-day hackathon at the University of Rwanda (Nyarugenge Campus) for 100 students/young professionals: hands-on tool sessions in TensorFlow, Data Science, Cybersecurity, and Git plus Nano-Mentorship, building open-source prototypes for East African challenges across three tracks:
- **Track 1: AgriTech & Climate Resilience** (Data Science & AI) — smallholder farming, unpredictable weather, crop diseases via predictive dashboards or lightweight TensorFlow models.
- **Track 2: FinTech & Financial Inclusion** (Data Science & Cybersecurity) — mobile money fraud and social engineering via transaction anomaly detection or security verification tools.
- **Track 3: Smart Cities & Public Resource Allocation** (Data Science) — Kigali public-service route planning / peak-hour traffic using transit and geospatial datasets.

## Context

- Host: IEEE CMU Africa; sponsors: CMU Africa IEEE Student Branch, IEEE CS Micro Mentoring, University of Rwanda IEEE; ~78 participants.
- **Only students in Rwanda are eligible** (final pitch showcase is in-person at the University of Rwanda on Sep 5, 2026); students only; teams of 3–4.
- Deadline Sep 3, 2026 @ 11:45pm CAT. Submission: project title & summary, public GitHub repo (all source, data scripts, commit history), pitch deck (PDF/PPTX), 45-second video demo.
- Judging (100 pts): Local Impact & Problem Alignment 20; Technical Execution & Tool Use 35; MVP & Working Prototype 20 (no slide-only submissions); Innovation & Resourcefulness 15; Pitch Quality & Time Management 10.
- Prize (non-cash): Hackathon Champion — signed IEEE Computer Society winner certificate + 1-on-1 VIP mentorship with an IEEE Distinguished Technologist or industry leader.
