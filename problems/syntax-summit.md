## Problem statement

Invent. Compile. Inspire. Don't just write code — create the language itself: design and build a Domain-Specific Language (DSL) that simplifies solving problems within a specific domain (AI, robotics, finance, healthcare, education, cybersecurity, game development, automation, IoT, or any specialized field), making complex tasks more intuitive, expressive, and efficient. Submissions may include a custom language/DSL, a parser/interpreter/transpiler/compiler, documentation of syntax and purpose, sample programs, and a real-world use case.

## Context

- Online, public; 6 non-cash prizes; event window Jul 18, 2026 – Jan 14, 2027; deadline Jan 14, 2027 @ 5:00pm PKT (7:00am EST).
- Eligibility: above legal age of majority in country of residence; students only; companies/professional organizations excluded; all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions. Solo or teams.
- Submission: project repository; working demonstration video ≤ 5 min (language in action, key features, how users interact).
- Judging: Innovation & Originality 25 pts (novel idea, unique/meaningful problem, forward-thinking); Technical Implementation 25 pts (quality/complexity, parser/interpreter/compiler/transpiler correctness); Language Design 20 pts (intuitive, consistent syntax; fits target domain); Real-World Impact 15 pts (adoption/extension potential); Documentation & Presentation 10 pts (clarity, ease of setup/usage); User Experience 5 pts (developer experience).
- Prizes: Best User Experience; Most Innovative DSL; Best Compiler; Best Interpreter; Future of Programming Award; Best Technical Implementation (all non-cash).
- Host: Student Organization (contact via COMSATS University Lahore). Judges include Cruze co-founder/CEO and an Oracle America Consulting Member of Technical Staff.
