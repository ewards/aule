## Problem statement

AI for Mental/Physical Health and Concussion Recovery. Hack for Humanity is a 1-month hackathon that brings developers together to come up with unique and creative solutions to mental and physical health issues, with an optional focus on AI — use technology to make meaningful changes in humanity by addressing health concerns. You must build software (an app, website, or game) that improves mental or physical well-being. Core categories: Mental Health and Physical Health; dedicated tracks: Best Use of AI/ML & Responsible AI (projects implementing ML), Best Tech for Concussion Recovery (brain injury recovery), plus Best Design, Girls Who Code Future Innovator Award, Best Use of Render, Best Innovation and Creativity, and Public Voting Winner.

## Context

- Online, public; $100 cash + 13 non-cash prize tracks (subscriptions, credits, domains, certificates); deadline Sep 4, 2026 @ 11:45pm EDT.
- 1-month event; work individually or in teams of 2–4; prior experience not required, all skill levels welcome.
- Submission: GitHub repository with functioning source code; video max 4 min covering (1) the issue solved, (2) the software and its functionality in detail, (3) an overview of how it was created.
- Judging: judged on categories including Design and Innovation and Creativity; award criteria include Domain Effectiveness, Feasibility & Safety, Innovation & Novelty, and UI/UX & Accessibility.
- Prizes: Best Mental Health Tool $100 cash + sponsor stack; Best Physical Health Tool (~$506 value); Responsible AI (~$8,676 value: SSOJet licenses + more); Best Design; Best Use of Render (3 winners, up to $500 Render credits); Best Tech for Concussion Recovery; Girls Who Code Future Innovator (≥ 50% student team); Best Use of AI/ML; Best Innovation and Creativity; Public Voting Winner; participation perks for registrers; referral prizes.
- Eligibility: ages 13+; all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions.
- Partners: Girls Who Code, Concussion Alliance, YOUTH x AI, Rutgers/Johns Hopkins/Pitt/UW Synapse, Sprqs, SparkCloud.
