## Problem statement

Biology needs builders. Theme: Artificial Intelligence for Human Health. Design and build a meaningful technical product that helps people detect illness earlier, understand it better, or live with it more easily — an app, website, simulation, system, or algorithm (e.g. an AI EdTech app teaching specific health concepts, a classification model trained on imaging datasets, or a novel medical innovation proposal). AI assistants are encouraged while building; the bar is that you understand what you built and can walk judges through your own code.
