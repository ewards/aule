## Problem statement

"Build. Pitch. Win Cash." Aspiring founders die not from bad ideas but from the gap between "I have an idea" and "I built something real." Create a product, platform, or tool that solves a real problem the way a startup would — with genuine market potential, a clear user base, and the basic structure of a business you could actually grow. Focus areas: Problem Solvers (retail, food, transportation, housing pain points), Consumer Apps, B2B Tools (for small businesses, creators, startups), Underserved Markets.

## Context

- Host: Student Organization (online, public); deadline Oct 15, 2026 @ 5:00pm EDT; ~144 participants.
- Eligibility: ages 13–22 only (middle school, high school, college students); companies/professional organizations excluded; all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions; teams of 1–5.
- $275 cash: 1st $150, 2nd $75, 3rd $50.
- Submission: 5-minute video covering The Problem, The Build (codebase/architecture/stack walkthrough), The Demo (real-time screen share), and Scalability.
- Judging (25% each): Technical Execution (works, grasp of stack); Innovation & UX (original idea, intuitive interface); Business & Finance (realistic business model, revenue streams, path to sustainability); Communication (deep understanding of product, architecture, target consumers).
