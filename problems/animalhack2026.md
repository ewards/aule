## Problem statement

Demonstrate your talent and showcase your hack for animals, animal welfare, and human-animal relationships. Student-led international, fully online hackathon (4th edition, 2023–2026): identify animal-related needs and challenges and develop a creative, technology-based solution — projects may focus on pets, service/assistance animals, farm animals, wildlife, or the environments in which animals live (welfare and quality of life; pet owners/caregivers/vets; behavior, enrichment, training, communication; shelters/rescues/foster; adoption matching; lost-pet reunion; environment monitoring; responsible agriculture/aquaculture; humane food-system innovation; wildlife conservation; human-wildlife coexistence; abuse/poaching/trafficking prevention; service-animal accessibility; public education). Solutions can be apps, games, platforms, devices, sensors, robots, computer vision, data analysis, predictive models, visualization, educational resources, or 3D modeling/printing.

## Context

- Online, public; 12 non-cash prizes (certificates + book-chapter publication); flexible project period Jun 14 – Sep 12, 2026; submission deadline Sep 12, 2026 @ 12:00pm EDT (page text says EST); online presentations Sep 13, 2026 (10 min each incl. ~3 min Q&A).
- Eligibility: open to all ages and technical backgrounds per organizer (Devpost tags: above legal age of majority, students only, companies excluded); individuals and teams; free entry; no prior hackathon experience required.
- Submission (all 3 required, firm deadlines): project submitted on Devpost by Sep 12 12:00pm; presentation registration via Google Form (presenter, country/timezone, title, one-paragraph abstract, public link to actual outcome — code repo, storage, live app) by Sep 12 12:00pm; deliver online presentation on Sep 13. Unverifiable work = no scheduled presentation.
- Judging: Impact (quality/quantity of impact; big vs small problem; many vs few helped); Creativity & Innovation (originality, new or creatively combined ideas, significant improvement); Execution (developed into functional, demonstrable, or well-supported outcome); Presentation (clear explanation of problem, importance, solution, results).
- Prizes: 15+ awards — Grand Prize, 2nd, 3rd, 3× Honorable Mention, and Excellence in Innovation/Research/Creativity/Entrepreneurship/Social Impact/Teamwork, Emerging Talent, Key Contributor; each: certificate of achievement + invitation to publish a book chapter in the Binnovative Innovation Book series (KDP).
- Host: AnimalHack (student-led), supported by Binnovative (Massachusetts nonprofit). Organizers: Naomi McCreary, Hanna Suzuki, Alyssa Yasuhara, Rimi Yoshikawa. Judges: Yuma Karube, Banri Ouchi, Sarasa Ouchi, AnimalHack Advisory Board.
