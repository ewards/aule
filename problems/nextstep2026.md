## Problem statement

Breaking barriers, one idea at a time — theme: **Earth Forward**. Our planet is facing unprecedented environmental challenges — climate change and pollution, biodiversity loss, resource depletion. Identify a pressing environmental problem affecting your community or the world and develop a creative technological solution: reducing waste, improving renewable energy, conserving water, protecting wildlife, monitoring ecosystems, advancing sustainable agriculture, or helping communities adapt to climate change. Encouraged to collaborate with local organizations, environmental groups, researchers, or community members so the solution addresses real-world needs.
