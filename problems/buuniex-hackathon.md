## Problem statement

Build innovative AI solutions that solve real-world problems. Open-ended AI innovation challenge (any domain or use case) across: generative AI applications (assistants, content generation, RAG), computer vision (image/video analysis, detection, recognition), NLP (search, text analysis, translation, summarization), healthcare & education (accessibility, learning, well-being), sustainability & social impact, productivity & automation, AI developer tools (coding assistants, debugging, testing), or any original AI idea with real-world value.

## Context

- Online, public; 2 non-cash prizes; event window Jun 22 – Sep 22, 2026; deadline Sep 22, 2026 @ 5:00pm IST (7:30am EDT).
- Eligibility: above legal age of majority in country of residence; students only; companies/professional organizations excluded; all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions.
- Submission must clearly demonstrate idea, implementation, and impact: project title & description; problem statement (real-world problem and why it matters); solution & features (approach, key features, how AI is used); technology stack (AI models, APIs, frameworks, libraries); demo (working link, deployed app, or video); source code (GitHub repo, complete code); impact & future scope.
- Judging: Innovation (how unique and creative the idea is) — the published rubric lists only Innovation; submission guidance emphasizes creativity, technical implementation, and potential real-world impact.
- Prizes: AI Credits Jackpot — winner gets $300 worth of Featherless AI credits; Learn to Invest Prize — 2nd-place team gets a premium Stock Market & Investing course (fundamental/technical analysis, portfolio and risk management).
- Host: TechieBunnies team. Sponsor: Featherless AI.
