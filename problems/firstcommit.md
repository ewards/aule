## Problem statement

Your first project. Your first commit. Your future. FirstCommit is a beginner-friendly hackathon with no required theme: build web applications, mobile apps, desktop applications, games, hardware projects, AI-powered tools, or any other software-related project — something you're passionate about. Learning is valued over perfection; AI tools are allowed and encouraged as learning aids (disclose significant AI assistance), but they should support your work, not replace it.

- Eligibility: ages 13–21 only; students only; companies/professional organizations excluded; all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions. Solo or teams.
