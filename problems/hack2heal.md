## Problem statement

"Build anything. Solve for health." Hack2Heal 2.0 is a global healthcare innovation hackathon for students, researchers, developers, designers, and innovators: transform bold ideas into solutions with meaningful healthcare impact. Round 1 is idea submission only (no prototype required); 20 teams are shortlisted for a Final Round to develop and showcase. Interdisciplinary ideas welcome: AI & ML, biotechnology, biology, physics, chemistry, blockchain, data science, digital health, medical technology.

## Context

- Host: Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM), online; deadline Sep 10, 2026 @ 11:45pm IST; ~115 participants; registration free.
- Eligibility: above legal age of majority; students only; companies excluded; all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions.
- $100 cash for the Final Round winner; Top Performers: opportunity for a Scopus-indexed research publication (3); Impactful Showcases: research/internship opportunity at IEM Institute of Fundamental Research (IIFR, 5).
- Round 1 submission: official Hack2Heal 2.0 presentation template (mandatory format), PDF on Devpost — problem statement, proposed solution, innovation & technology, target users & impact, implementation approach, team details.
- Judging: Innovation & Originality; Problem Relevance; Impact Potential; Feasibility & Scalability; Technical & Scientific Approach; Clarity & Presentation.
