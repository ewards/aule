## Problem statement

Build the most creative and visually stunning 3D website imaginable. No limits. Just creativity. Create the most visually stunning, immersive, and imaginative 3D website possible — no restrictions on theme, industry, or purpose: a futuristic city, a digital art gallery, an interactive story, a sci-fi experience, a fantasy world, a product showcase, a game-like website, or something nobody has ever seen before. Most hackathons focus on solving problems; this one focuses on creating wonder — projects that make people stop scrolling and say "Wow."

## Context

- Online, public; $55 in cash prizes; deadline Aug 31, 2026 @ 11:45pm CDT (Sep 1, 12:45am EDT).
- Beginner-friendly; open to anyone interested in web design, 3D graphics, digital art, UI/UX, creative coding, interactive experiences.
- Allowed: AI tools and assistants, online assets and models, open-source libraries, tutorials; Three.js, React Three Fiber, Babylon.js, Blender, Spline, WebGL, WebGPU, GSAP, Framer Motion. Technical complexity is optional — a beautiful, creative project can outperform a highly technical one.
- Requirements: web-based project with meaningful 3D or immersive visual elements; appropriate for all audiences; solo or team.
- Submission: publicly accessible website link; project description (idea and inspiration); at least 3 screenshots; short demo video 1–5 min (optional); list of technologies/tools used; source code.
- Judging: Visual Design (polish, composition, color, lighting, typography, animation, presentation); Creativity & Originality (unique concept, fresh and memorable); User Experience & Interactivity (enjoyability, navigation, responsiveness, engagement, quality of interactions).
- Prizes: 1st $30, 2nd $15, 3rd $10 (gift cards + certificates).
- Eligibility: above legal age of majority; students only; companies/professional organizations excluded. Host: Tanishq Kumar.
