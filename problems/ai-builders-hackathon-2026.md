## Problem statement

Building the Future of Intelligent Systems. The Internet Needs Better AI. The world doesn't need another AI demo — most AI projects never move beyond a prototype, a landing page, or a collection of prompts. What's needed are AI products that solve real problems, save time, create value, and earn a place in people's daily lives. Build an AI product that solves a real problem: an AI application, autonomous agent, multi-agent system, developer tool, workflow automation platform, research assistant, productivity tool, education solution, healthcare application, business software, creative tool, or an entirely new category of AI-powered product. Not looking for pitch decks, concept videos, or AI wrappers with minimal differentiation — the test is "I would use this tomorrow."

## Context

- Online, public; $33,900+ in prizes (cash + sponsor awards); deadline Sep 15, 2026 @ 11:00pm EDT.
- Eligibility: above legal age of majority; students only; companies/professional organizations excluded; all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions.
- Submission: project submission form (team info, name, description, links); working product (functional prototype/judges can evaluate); public GitHub repo with documentation and setup instructions; demo video up to 5 min (problem, how it works, key features, role of AI, live demo); presentation deck up to 10 slides (problem, solution, target users, features, architecture, AI tech, impact, roadmap).
- Judging: Innovation & Creativity 20%; Technical Implementation 25% (AI models, agents, workflows, system architecture); Problem Solving & Impact 25%; User Experience & Design 15%; Presentation & Demo 15%.
- Prizes: Best SaaS Product $4,000; NexFellow Founder's Choice (3 months Founder Plan); NexFellow Product Excellence (2 months); NexFellow Innovation (1 month); Tin Computer Credits $299 × 100 teams (first-come, 60-day claim window).
- Host: OSC; sponsors: NexFellow, Algoverse, Tin Computer, Sylus Ai.
