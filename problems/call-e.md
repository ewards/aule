## Problem statement

Turn your code into an AI agent that makes real phone calls. CALL-E is a developer-first platform for building AI agents that make real phone calls and get real-world tasks done: your agents can plan a call, dial out, hold a natural conversation, adapt in real time, and return structured results — all through CALL-E's SDK, API, and MCP. Build and deploy a functional application that uses CALL-E to solve a real business or project problem — customer outreach, appointment scheduling, lead qualification, service coordination, workflow automation, or something no one's tried yet.

## Context

- Online, public; $10,000 in cash prizes; deadline Sep 14, 2026 @ 11:45pm SGT.
- All new CALL-E accounts come with 20 free calls; more can be requested via a form.
- Submission: open a pull request to CALL-E's public repo (github.com/CALLE-AI/awesome-phone-call-agents) into the correct Contribution Area, provide the PR URL on the Devpost form, a ~3-min public demo video (YouTube/Vimeo), the email of your CALL-E account; optional live demo URL and CALL-E Feedback Survey (enters for five "Most Valuable Feedback" prizes).
- Judging: Real World Impact (real, specific phone-work problem, credible solution worth building further); Quality of the Idea (creative, non-obvious use of CALL-E; clear, well-scoped, reusable contribution); Technical Implementation (genuine, working, non-trivial use of CALL-E — imported and actually called at runtime, not just referenced); Product Experience & Demo (complete coherent experience, demo video clearly communicates what it does and why it matters).
- Prizes: Most Practical Use Case $4,000; Most Innovative Use Case $3,000; Honorable Mention $1,000 × 2; Most Valuable Feedback $200 × 5 (plus meetings, blog features, and CALL-E credits).
